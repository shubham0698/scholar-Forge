ScholarForge Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

ScholarForge ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our academic community, including student researchers, faculty guides, peer reviewers, and university administrators. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your personal information and research submissions when you access our open-access platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide academic publishing, peer review, and digital certification services:

  • Account Profile Data: Full name, academic email address, selected role (Student, Guide, Reviewer), institution/college name, and academic department.
  • Research Submissions: Uploaded manuscript PDF files, titles, abstracts, domain keywords, guide details, and contributor affiliations.
  • Digital Certificate Records: Unique Certificate IDs, QR verification links, issue dates, and Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs).
  • Analytics & Readership Metrics: Aggregated paper views, PDF downloads, category performance, and technical log data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate academic and platform operations:

  • Facilitating peer review evaluations and plagiarism similarity checks.
  • Generating cryptographically verifiable digital PDF certificates with QR codes.
  • Indexing open-access research via standard OAI-PMH XML metadata protocols.
  • Sending account verification codes (OTP) and editorial status notifications.
  • Maintaining platform security and preventing unauthorized access or plagiarism.

4. Open Access & Data Sharing

ScholarForge is an open-access academic repository. Papers marked as "Public" and approved by peer review are made publicly accessible worldwide under author copyright. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or contact details to third-party advertisers.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. All user passwords are encrypted using secure pbkdf2:sha256 hashing algorithms. Database records and certificate files are maintained in secure data environments.

6. Your Rights & Choices

As a registered author or reviewer, you have the right to:

  • Access and update your profile information via the Dashboard.
  • Toggle manuscript visibility between Public and Private.
  • Request complete deletion of your account and associated private drafts.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data processing practices, please contact our Editorial Support team at support@scholarforge.com or visit our Contact Page.
